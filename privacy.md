# Privacy policy

This blog does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. Visit counts are measured with
[GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com), which records anonymous page views (page, referrer, browser, country)
without cookies or personal identifiers.

Links to the App Store include a campaign tag so we can see which article led to a download; Apple reports these
numbers to us only in aggregate.

For the {{ site.app_name }} app's own privacy policy, see its App Store page. Questions: contact us via the App Store page.
