# About {{ site.app_name }}

Most fitness apps are built for people who already know what they're doing. {{ site.app_name }} is built for everyone else: people who are new to the gym, or coming back after a long break, and want a simple plan instead of a wall of options.

The app keeps it to the essentials: build a workout, train with a rest timer, log your meals and macros, and follow your weight trend. The guides on this site explain the basics in plain language, so you can walk into the gym knowing what to do.

{{ site.app_name }} is available on iPhone. Android is coming later.
